# Why it misses
They queue work at different points in the loop — mixing them up is a common source of ordering bugs.

<Steps>
  <Step icon="flag" title="Two different queues">
    <code>process.nextTick()</code> queues a callback for right after the current operation, before the <kw>event loop</kw> continues.
  </Step>
  <Step icon="clock" title="nextTick runs first">
    The nextTick queue is drained completely before the loop moves to timers, I/O, or immediates — even before <flow>promise microtasks</flow> in older Node versions.
  </Step>
  <Step icon="loop" title="setImmediate waits its turn">
    <code>setImmediate()</code> schedules a callback for the check phase, after the current poll phase completes.
  </Step>
  <Step icon="gear" title="Why it matters">
    Overusing <code>nextTick</code> can <bad>starve the event loop</bad>, since it keeps deferring the next phase until its queue is empty.
  </Step>
</Steps>
