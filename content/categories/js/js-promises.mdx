# Why it misses
They differ in how they handle rejection and what value they resolve with — choosing the wrong one silently swallows errors or crashes early.

<Steps>
  <Step icon="check" title="Promise.all">
    Resolves when <Flow>all</Flow> promises resolve. Rejects immediately if <Bad>any</Bad> one rejects — the first rejection wins.
  </Step>
  <Step icon="flag" title="Promise.allSettled">
    Resolves when <Flow>all</Flow> promises settle (resolve or reject). Returns an array of <code>&#123;status, value&#125;</code> or <code>&#123;status, reason&#125;</code> objects.
  </Step>
  <Step icon="clock" title="Promise.race">
    Resolves or rejects with the <Flow>first</Flow> settled promise — whether it resolved or rejected.
  </Step>
  <Step icon="key" title="When to use which">
    Use <code>all</code> when all results are required. Use <code>allSettled</code> when you want partial results. Use <code>race</code> for timeouts or fallbacks.
  </Step>
</Steps>
