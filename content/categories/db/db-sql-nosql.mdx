# Why it misses
The real distinction is about data shape and consistency guarantees, not scale or how 'modern' a project is.

<Steps>
  <Step icon="box" title="Schema shape">
    SQL databases enforce a <kw>fixed schema</kw> with relations between tables; most NoSQL stores allow flexible, often nested, document shapes.
  </Step>
  <Step icon="key" title="Consistency model">
    Relational databases typically guarantee strong <flow>ACID consistency</flow>; many NoSQL stores trade some of that for availability and horizontal scale.
  </Step>
  <Step icon="net" title="Query patterns">
    SQL excels at complex joins across normalized data; document and key-value stores excel when you mostly fetch a whole record by key.
  </Step>
  <Step icon="check" title="The actual decision">
    Pick based on how <good>relational the data is</good> and what consistency guarantees the domain needs — not on perceived modernity.
  </Step>
</Steps>
